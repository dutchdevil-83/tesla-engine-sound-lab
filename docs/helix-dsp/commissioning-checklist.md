# HELIX DSP commissioning checklist

**Do not change live DSP settings under this document alone.** Work with the qualified installer and preserve a restorable backup. Record actual measurements; blank values mean unverified.

1. [ ] Record vehicle speaker inventory and installed drivers.
2. [ ] Install the remaining passenger-side woofer (separate authorization/installer work).
3. [ ] Separate tweeter wiring for independent DSP channels; confirm wiring before amplifier enable.
4. [ ] Inventory every physical HELIX input/output and identify connected vehicle signals.
5. [ ] Export and checksum current DSP PC-Tool configuration; confirm restore procedure.
6. [ ] Verify electrical connections, polarity labels and amplifier channel connections with power isolated by installer procedure.
7. [ ] Record input sensitivity and gains from measurement; do not infer values.
8. [ ] Configure speaker-specific high/low-pass filters only from manufacturer data and acoustic measurement.
9. [ ] Protect tweeters with validated high-pass before enabling their amplifier channels.
10. [ ] Configure and verify original Tesla input routing.
11. [ ] Add optical Pi input only after bench-safe signal limits and routing tests.
12. [ ] Configure DIRECTOR digital source volume and safe startup level; verify model/firmware compatibility.
13. [ ] Verify Tesla warning and alert sounds are audible, unmodified and uninterrupted.
14. [ ] Measure speaker polarity and phase.
15. [ ] Measure and configure time alignment.
16. [ ] Perform acoustic measurements at documented seat/microphone positions.
17. [ ] Apply EQ based on recorded measurements and agreed target curve.
18. [ ] Validate frequency response and speaker balance at safe levels.
19. [ ] Verify simultaneous Tesla music and engine sound; test isolated digital mute.
20. [ ] Export final DSP file, measurements, firmware/software versions and restoration steps.
