# ADR-002: Verify HELIX mixing before buying digital hardware

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-31
- **Requirements:** REQ-AUD-001, REQ-DSP-001

The V TWELVE DSP MK2 product documentation lists optical S/PDIF and DSP PC-Tool documents digital routing, but installed model and live preset behavior are unknown. Defer purchase of Pi S/PDIF hardware until the exact unit demonstrates continuous analog Tesla plus digital source mixing, independent digital mute/volume, and preserved warning audio. DIRECTOR digital volume is not a Raspberry Pi software command.
